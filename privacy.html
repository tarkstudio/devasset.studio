Section 1

Entity & Operational Architecture

1.1. Platform Identity. DevAsset Studio is an independent developer asset generation suite engineered and operated under parent studio TarkStudio (led by Brijesh as founder and lead developer), based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India.

1.2. Operational Nature. DevAsset Studio is a developer-focused utility application that enables users to generate app icons (512×512), feature banners, Play Store screenshot mockups (9:16 portrait frames), App Store Optimization ("ASO") metadata, and legal policy assets, with all generation and rendering performed directly within the user's browser RAM and local client storage. DevAsset Studio is a tool, not a hosting service, marketplace, or publishing intermediary.

1.3. Statutory Framework. This Policy is drafted and published with the intention of full compliance with: the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA") and rules framed thereunder; the Information Technology Act, 2000, including Section 43A (compensation for failure to protect data) and Section 79 (intermediary due diligence, applied here voluntarily and to the extent relevant to the Platform's tooling function); the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules"); and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 ("IT Rules 2021"). Where a User accesses the Platform from the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, DevAsset Studio additionally aligns its practices with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") as a matter of voluntary best practice.

Section 2

Definitions & Interpretation

  1. "Platform" means the DevAsset Studio web application, including all of its generation, mockup, export, and metadata-synthesis utilities.
  2. "Source Material" means any app screenshot, raw imagery, logo, UI design, draft text, or other file a User uploads, imports, or drags into the Platform for processing.
  3. "Generated Asset(s)" means any app icon, banner, mockup, screenshot frame, caption, ASO metadata string, or policy draft produced by the Platform from Source Material or User input.

Section 4

Local Client-Side Execution & Zero Image Harvesting

Core Guarantee

DevAsset Studio is architected on a principle of 100% Client-Side, In-Memory Processing. Every app screenshot, raw image, logo, UI design, and draft caption you provide is processed strictly within volatile local memory on your own device — your browser's RAM, the HTML5 Canvas rendering surface, and local IndexedDB/Blob storage. At no point in the ordinary operation of the Platform does your Source Material leave your device or reach a DevAsset Studio server.

4.1. No Backend Image Servers. DevAsset Studio does not operate, maintain, or route Source Material through any backend image-storage server. There is no server-side upload endpoint for screenshots, logos, or raw imagery in the ordinary course of the Platform's mockup, banner, icon, or ASO-generation functionality.

4.2. Zero Retention, Zero Review, Zero Model Training. DevAsset Studio does not retain copies of your Source Material after your session ends, does not view or manually inspect the content of your uploads, and does not use your Source Material, Generated Assets, or any derivative thereof to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any artificial intelligence or machine-learning model, whether operated by DevAsset Studio or any third party.

4.3. Verification Note. Because all processing described above occurs client-side, you may independently verify this architecture using your browser's own network-inspection tools (for example, the "Network" tab of your browser's developer console) to confirm that Source Material is not transmitted off-device during normal use of the mockup and export features.

Section 5

Session Ephemerality & Data Purge

5.1. All canvas buffers, resized bitmaps, intermediate render layers, and generated Asset Bundles created during your session exist only for the duration of that session and are purged automatically upon the earliest of: (i) closing the browser tab or window running the Platform; (ii) clearing your browser cache, IndexedDB, or site data for the Platform's domain; or (iii) completion of your Asset Bundle download, where the Platform's implementation clears the in-memory buffer immediately following a successful export.

5.2. Because no server-side copy of your Source Material or Generated Assets is created under Section 4, DevAsset Studio has no ability to retrieve, restore, or reproduce your work product following such purge. You are solely responsible for downloading and independently backing up any Asset Bundle you wish to retain before closing your session.

5.3. Any browser-local persistence used by the Platform to support in-progress work (such as IndexedDB caching between page reloads within an active session) is used exclusively to preserve your own workflow continuity on your own device, and remains subject to erasure by you at any time through your browser's own site-data controls.
